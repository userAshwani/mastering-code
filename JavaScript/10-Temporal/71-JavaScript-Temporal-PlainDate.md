**Temporal.PlainDate stores a calendar date without a time or time zone.**

</div>

---

## Quick Summary

- PlainDate stores year, month, and day.
- It does not store time.
- It does not store a time zone.
- It is useful for birthdays and calendar dates.

---

## Key Points

| Feature | Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| Year | Calendar year. |
| Month | Calendar month. |
| Day | Day of the month. |
| Time | Not included. |

---

---

## Remember

- Use PlainDate for date-only values.
- No clock time is included.
- No time zone is included.
- It represents a calendar date.
